KZ evidence-control-3 / 0.3.2 — 2026-10-16

Purpose
Repair the review failures identified in the payments/layout/fin audit before
running a fresh, bounded 10-task KZ-on-DSH campaign. The historical source and
three-task results are retained separately.

Algorithm changes
1. Global accept no longer closes all open gaps. Each gap requires an explicit
   ID, an explanation and passing receipts targeted to that gap before execution.
   Planning-stage gaps persist even if the later verdict accepts. Distinct gaps
   remain distinct, including multiple defects under one public requirement.
2. Execution success and correctness evidence are separate. Check expectations
   include author, provenance, expected rule, source quotes and independence.
   Task quotes and observed evidence references are checked. Self-consistency,
   missing sources and replaying an old receipt cannot resolve a correctness gap.
   Evidence becoming stale reopens the relevant obligation.
3. Review checks are preflighted before execution. Unsupported heredocs, masked
   shell stages and composite commands return repair guidance. A concrete lack
   of information/capability can produce a zero-check revision without executing
   a check or spending a second verdict request.
4. Prompts require actual failure/recovery boundaries, independently sourced
   expectations, and rendering tied to the current artifact. Own readiness logs,
   same-container restarts and mocks skipping recovery do not establish the
   stronger properties claimed in the failed run.

Limits of these changes
Source-reference and closure checks enforce traceability and structure. They do
not prove model-attributed authorship, semantic independence, exhaustive task
coverage or domain correctness. Official task scoring remains the outcome.
No historical solution, corrected finance formula or hidden verifier answer was
added to the production algorithm. Regression fixtures are test-only.
