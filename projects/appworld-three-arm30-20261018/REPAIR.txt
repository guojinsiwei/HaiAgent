AppWorld budget and accounting repair, 2026-10-19

Scope: HLD, KZDSH-new, Recuris and DSH, same original 30 tasks.
Limits remain 8 CNY per attempt and 720 CNY for the entire campaign including all earlier attempts and retries.
No algorithm, task instructions, official grading, model, or task selection changes.

Repairs:
1. KZ internal cost accounting now prices cache reads at 0.04 CNY / million tokens, ordinary input/cache writes at 2, output at 8, matching the existing shared ledger.
2. Conservative maximum-request reservations and unknown-usage charges remain enforced. Shared persistent accounting continues to enforce the total cap.
3. Budget RPC timeout is 35 seconds (database lock wait is 20 seconds). Up to two retries reuse the same lease/request body. Model generations are not replayed by this repair. Explicit budget denial is never retried.
4. Read-only ledger snapshots use deferred transactions. Closed response sockets do not produce controller failures; committed transactions remain available for idempotent replay.
5. Budget stops are classified separately from solver crashes. Official grades are retained even when the solver exits nonzero. Original raw trial summaries remain unchanged.

Restart procedure:
New solver entries wait before model calls. Existing solvers finish and are graded. Once all six slots are waiting with zero requests, the old controller is stopped. Those zero-call folders are archived under repair-v1/parked-zero-call-attempts.
All completed logs, worlds and official grades are retained. Retry attempts use new job IDs ending --repair1. Only unpassed trials affected by old cache overcount or budget RPC failure enter the repair queue, ahead of original unstarted tasks.
The same spending.sqlite is retained. Only authorized retry job IDs are appended to its profile; previous charges remain counted.

Verification:
13 offline tests cover cached pricing, strict monetary limits, uncertain usage, dropped HTTP responses, idempotence, historical classification, retry selection, and controller continuation with nonzero previous spending and an isolated trial failure.
Four complete solver entries were tested in isolated containers with dummy keys and network disabled; all were denied at the budget gate before model dispatch.
Runtime source hashes and deployment changes are recorded in server repair-v1/ready.json and deployed.json.

Storage threshold remains unchanged: Docker filesystem >30 GiB and experiment filesystem >15 GiB to admit new trials.
